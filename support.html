Loglike Support

Found a bug, lost progress or have a question? Write to bablarumm@gmail.com. Add your device model and iOS version.

Progress on a new device

Progress syncs through iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and open the game on each one.

No sound

Check the silent switch and the volume. Sound and music can be turned on in the game Settings.

Colors are hard to tell apart

Turn on Colorblind mode in Settings. It uses its own heat colors.
