Loglike Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Loglike app ("the app") collects and why. The app is made by gottabeugly ("we").

Data we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts. We do not run our own servers or analytics.

Your game progress

Your game progress (shards, upgrades, awards, settings and the current run) is stored on your device.

If cloud saves are available, a copy of the same progress is also kept in your own cloud account so it can follow you to another device: iCloud on iOS (if you are signed in to iCloud), Google Play Games Saved Games on Android (if you are signed in to Play Games). This copy is stored by Apple or Google under your account. We cannot see it and it is not sent to us. You can turn it off in the iCloud settings of your device or by signing out of Play Games.

Deleting the app deletes the progress on the device. The cloud copy can be removed in your iCloud or Play Games settings.

Advertising and tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier.

Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the app sends your best level to that service so it can appear on leaderboards. Apple and Google handle this data under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
